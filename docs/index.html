Our mission

In the AI era, talking to your computer is a basic need — and basic needs should never be gated by payment. Don't pay companies $144 a year for your own voice: dictation here is free for everyone, on-device, MIT-licensed, forever. The only optional extra, AI cleanup, is bring-your-own-key — pay the model provider cents for tokens, not a middleman for permission.

Honestly, we think every operating system should ship this as a default feature, and the day that happens well enough, apps like this one become unnecessary. Until then, we build it in the open — MIT-licensed and free for everyone, forever.

Questions

Really free? What's the catch?

MIT-licensed, no account, no telemetry. Your Mac provides the compute — there is nothing to sell you.

How accurate is it?

Whisper on-device, models from tiny to large. Your personal dictionary fixes names and jargon once, forever.

Does AI cleanup send my text to the cloud?

Cleanup is Off by default, so the raw on-device transcript is pasted as-is. Turn on cleanup and pick OpenRouter (one key, any model), a direct provider — OpenAI, Anthropic, or Groq — with your own key, or local Ollama for fully on-device. Audio never goes anywhere, regardless.

Which Macs?

macOS 14 Sonoma or newer. Apple Silicon flies; Intel works with smaller models.

Where does my data live?

Settings and history stay in Application Support on your Mac, and API keys live in the macOS Keychain. Uninstall the app and remove those to erase every trace.
